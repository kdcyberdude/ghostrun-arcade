## 3D presentation

The renderer uses original Blender-built meshes depicting Nintendo character and object designs; this does not grant rights to those designs. Material textures, HDRIs, scanned vegetation and the source trees for rendered cards come from Poly Haven under CC0. The runtime and tool inventory is in [the 3D asset manifest](visual3d/ASSET_MANIFEST.json); named source authors and URLs are retained in [the asset attribution](visual3d/blender/ATTRIBUTION.md) and [Poly Haven manifest](visual3d/polyhaven-manifest.json).

The Press Start 2P font is distributed under SIL OFL 1.1. Three.js, glTF-Transform and meshoptimizer retain their respective MIT notices. Blender is an authoring tool and is not included in the browser download.
