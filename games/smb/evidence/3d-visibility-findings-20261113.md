# Native 3D visibility instrumentation

The immutable e521a55 replay passes all six expanded state/audio comparisons on all 32 original entrances and 146,036 input frames. This predates later flag/music and player-task integration. Its placement audit checks every frame and reports 43 frames in two groups; it does not prove pixel visibility, lighting, flicker or every z-fighting case.

The 42 bubble warnings arise from matching the padded 8×8 CHR container against a 4×4 source glyph. Closely spaced bubbles then exchange nearest neighbors. Measuring the actual source PNG alpha footprint removes all 42 warnings in a 6,600-frame 2-2 replay. The remaining 7-3 warning at frame 2457 comes from two Cheeps at (147,33) and (143,32): greedy foot matching exchanges their cartoon models. ActorPool already records each physical enemy owner. Exact source actor IDs prevent this exchange; the 3,184-frame 7-3 audit then reports zero issues. The regression preserves detection of a real displacement and leaves the 6-pixel tolerance unchanged. These are measurement corrections, not fitted level rules or game edits.

`visibility.ts` optionally measures actual world/HUD depth and alpha contribution. Four RGBA render targets retain 12 object masks per pass; each RGB channel represents one object's contribution. Ordinary transparent blending therefore preserves a visible actor behind glass instead of assigning the whole pixel to the front surface. Alpha-tested texture holes, clipped geometry, skinning, instancing and actual depth/order are retained. RGBA8 quantizes contribution to 1/255. Unsupported non-mesh renderables are reported explicitly.

The pass temporarily substitutes unlit mask materials, then restores original materials, scene background/override, viewport/scissor/render target/read buffer, clear state, shadow update flags and render statistics in `finally`. Bounding-box/sphere caches touched by geometry inspection are restored. Simulation and meaningful scene draw-state hashes are compared before/after instrumentation. The pass does not alter simulation rules, pose sampling or source clipping.

GPU regressions cover two translucent layers, unlabelled glass over an actor, a fully opaque occluder, alpha-test holes, shared materials across 13 independently labelled objects, MRT batching and exception restoration. Four native samples (axe, retainer, close bubbles and overlapping Cheeps) report no visibility candidates, instrumentation changes or page errors. These are selected samples, not all-frame visual proof. At the prototype gate the complete suite passes 1,791 tests and TypeScript.

Example: `V25_PORT=5588 npx tsx tools/visual3d/visibility.ts --native=TRACES --entrances=ENTRANCES --samples=1-4:1979,1-4:2224,2-2:4534,7-3:2457 --out=/tmp/visibility.json`. Use `--every=120` for diagnostic sampling or `--every=1` for every-frame instrumentation. Every intervening simulation input is still rendered to preserve authored pose history. Receipts distinguish instrumented samples from rendered prefixes and full input coverage, record source/tool dirtiness, and retain candidate counts. Shape, lighting, temporal flicker and z-fighting require separate evidence.
