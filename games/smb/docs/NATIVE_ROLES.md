# Established native roles (black-box, inferred by correlation with the remake — never read from the ROM)

Use these instead of re-deriving. Verify against the trace if a result looks odd; add new roles here when a probe establishes them.

## Trace (`tools/fidelity-lab/native-trace.mjs`)
Row i = state after i+1 frames: world, stage, x (page*256+x within the area visit; pipe rooms start on their own page; 8-4 wrong corridors subtract 1024),
y (one byte, wraps above the screen: 253 = -3), vx (signed speed byte*256 + fraction; position advances by byte/16 px), vy, engine (8 = playing, 2 = pipe/area
change), mode, task (3 = playing), ram. Remake step f matches native row f-1 (one frame of input latency; native latency varies by one frame per jump).

## Enemy slots (RAM ranges for `--ram`: 0x0F-0x13, 0x16-0x1A, 0x1E-0x22, 0x6E-0x72, 0x87-0x8B, 0xCF-0xD3, 0x754-0x755)
Five slots; fields flag, id, state, page, x low, y, then player size. Native y + 24 = remake feet y. Ids: 0 koopa, 2 beetle, 3 redKoopa, 5 hammerBro, 6 goomba,
10 cheep, 12 podoboo, 13 piranha, 14/15 paratroopa, 17 lakitu. Groups spawn as one object 24 px apart when the leader reaches x-camera ~249. Native walkers face
Mario on activation (Paratroopa/Beetle); a stomped Paratroopa becomes a Koopa walking away.

## Lakitu (id 17) and Spiny egg (id 18), area `lakitu` (4-1, 6-1, 8-2; rules in `data/native-lakitu.json`, code `updateNativeLakitu` in `src/simulation.ts`, test `tests/native-lakitu.test.ts`)
Probe: `tools/fidelity-lab/area-probe.mjs` branching from the 4-1 route with the full RAM dumped each frame; **RAM 0x58+slot = enemy x speed byte (signed, 1/16 px per frame; correlation 0.95 with the trace dx, Lakitu position = running sum of it)**.
Lakitu appears at x = camera + ~303 (4-1 480, 6-1 512, 8-2 336) at native y 40 (remake feet 64) with speed -5. With d = Lakitu x - Mario x (integer positions, previous frame) the speed is a direct function of d (not integrated),
in three modes: **left** (Lakitu ahead of Mario): s = -(5 + (60-|d|)/4) (-5 while 60+ px ahead, -20 right above Mario); it turns to **chase** once d <= -57 (Mario outran it). **Chase**: s = base + F(d),
base = Mario speed class (48 when Mario's speed byte >= 26, 32 walking, ~5 stopped, mixed values between), F = (60+d)/4 behind Mario, min(15,(64-d)/4) ahead (peak 15 at d 0..4). When Lakitu is ~60 px ahead (d >= 60 running / 59 walking)
s drops by 1 per frame; when d falls back below that it restarts at base+1: a sawtooth (49 -> 30..32 at 40 px/frame Mario speed) that keeps Lakitu 62-65 px ahead of a runner. If Mario stops, s keeps dropping to 0, then Lakitu flips to
**left** (-5) and drifts back over Mario, and chases again 57 px behind (4-1 x 1842 stall: peak 128 px ahead, back at d -57 after 90 frames; the test reproduces it to 2 px). **Flee**: when Mario passes a fixed x (4-1 3320, 6-1 2710, 8-2 762) Lakitu goes to
state 1, speed -1 px/frame, no more eggs (removed natively at x < camera - 73).
Eggs: thrown on Lakitu's second row and then every 128 frames (not 120), at Lakitu's previous-row x, native y 32 (feet 56); the egg does not move in x: y0 32.5, vy -3, +1/8 per frame, cap 3.25 (rise 37 px in 24 frames, back at 32 after 53,
lands on the ground (y 184) 98 frames after the throw). Eggs of one level show six variants of the arc up to 3 px apart (hidden y fraction). On landing it becomes a Spiny (state 0) that walks 0.5 px/frame toward Mario. Eggs that land left of
camera - 73 vanish. Not modelled: the exact s of the mixed speed class (Mario 4..13), the 2-frame delay of the sawtooth reset, Mario moving left, a Spiny falling off a ledge.
## Hammer Bros (id 5) and thrown hammers, area `hammer` (3-1, 5-2, 7-1, 8-3, 8-4; roster and rules in `data/native-hammer-bros.json` made by `tools/derive-hammer-schedule.mjs`, code `bindNativeHammerBros` / `updateNativeHammerBro` in `src/simulation.ts`, test `tests/native-hammer-bros.test.ts`)
Probes: `tools/fidelity-lab/area-probe.mjs --ram 0-2047` over the route in 3-1, 5-2, 7-1, 8-3 (full RAM per frame). **Thrown hammers are NOT in the enemy slots**: they are nine misc objects, state RAM 0x2A+s (0x8F counting down to 0x81 over 14 frames, then 0x81), x low 0x93+s, y 0xDB+s (native top-left, world x low byte). Enemy state bit 3 (8) of a Bro is set while a hammer is in its hand.
**Patrol**: x += 0.25 px/frame, right while (frames since the area load) mod 128 < 64, left for the next 64; no walls, no Mario dependence, identical for all Bros of an area visit (the native clock restarts at each load: row of the first task-1 load row; 163 at the first play row of a level, 119 at control after a pipe). A Bro first shows at its spawn x on that clock without moving on its first row. Removed natively once x < camera - 17 (remake 48, unchanged).
**Spawn**: x is a multiple of 16 (the screenshot-era level specs are 16-32 px off), y is a floor top or mid-air (5-2 x1296 spawns at the apex of its fall); a Bro appears at x - camera ~296-301 (remake rule x < camera + 304); after a pipe load it appears 28 frames after the load (8-4).
**Jumps** (hidden random state: recorded per Bro, most Bros of a pair jump on their first row): strong v0 -6, weak -3 px/frame (rise 78 / 20 px; a weak jump falls through the row it left), gravity 61/256, fall cap 828/256, position then speed (probe fit, 44 px errors in 420 samples over 10 arcs; the old 6.125/0.234 fit missed 339).
**Throws**: the first cycle starts when the Bro is on screen (x - camera <= ~245: Mario ~133 px away on a scrolling screen), then a cycle every 49 frames in 5-2 and 3-1 and every 29 frames in 7-1/8-3/8-4 (worlds 1-5 / 6-8 assumed); some cycles are skipped (recorded). The hammer exists from the cycle start, follows the Bro at (+2, -10) of its native top-left for 14 frames, then flies toward Mario (1 px/frame in all four levels, the remake's 1.5 was wrong) with vy -2, +1/16 per frame (exact integer fit, 19 flights, no cap seen). A first cycle can create a hammer that is dropped at once (ON bit, no object): the visible throw is then one period later.
**Hidden-RNG behaviours (jump times and classes, skipped cycles) are recorded for this route only and are not predictive for another route.** Level-start/pipe clock constants `levelClock` 162 / `pipeClock` 22 start the remake's `broClock` (counted in `updateEnemies`); tools/campaign-compare.ts re-syncs it to the native rows (BROCLOCK=0 turns that off; a castle-exit timeline that ends earlier than native leaves it 15-42 frames ahead in 3-1/7-1 otherwise).
**Stomp score**: +1000 (three native stomps: 3-1, 5-2, 7-1 score digits; `stompScore` in `data/native-hammer-bros.json`). Fireball score, stomp chain and the Bro hitbox are unmeasured.

## Native frame counter and stomp contact (areas `enemyvert`, `stomp`, `stomp2`; `STOMPS=1|2` in tools/campaign-compare.ts; evidence/stomp2-findings-20261111.md; ADOPTED)
**RAM 0x09 is the native frame counter**: +1 per frame, restarted (0) by each area load, the first task-1 row of the LAST load before control (1-2/2-2/4-2/7-2 style levels load twice). It runs through the lives screen, so its value at the first control row is: level start 164, pipe rise 120, underground fall 41, water room fall 58, title start 153 and after a death the lives-screen length (intro rows - 3). Contact with Mario runs only on even values: 51 of 51 route bounces, 167 of 167 model-run bounces. The remake's copy is `broClock`, re-seeded per segment from the byte by campaign-compare.
**Route trace rows are CPU slices that drift through the native frame** by 0.5 cycle per row (period 59,561 rows). That makes the byte read one ahead of Mario's frame (rows ~500..~8,500 and again +59,561), shows bounces one row late (P2 rows, between Mario's motion and the contact test, rows ~8,500..~26,100 and again), and is the origin of the input-phase flip (`PHASES=1`: early from row ~8,000 to ~59,000, then again from ~67,800). PPU-clock model traces have none of this.
36 native stomps of the recorded route (bounce: Mario vy byte >= 1 -> <= -3 without an A press, with an enemy state change). The contact test runs only on every second area frame (even rows since the first task-1 load row of the visit): on such a row
the bounce vy -4 shows with Mario already moved down (stomp row N = check row), on the next odd row it shows with Mario already moved UP 4 (the odd-row pattern, 17 stomps, 11 of them on odd rows). A falling Mario stomps when feet
(native y + 32) are <= 10 px above a walking Goomba's feet (native y + 24) and <= 15 px above a Koopa/Red Koopa/Paratroopa/Beetle (Hammer Bro 14-15 in 2 samples), |dx| <= ~10.5; two Goombas in contact are both squashed on the same row (8-4 f75586/f75606).
The remake tests every frame with boxes of 10 px (Goomba, centre of the native distribution) and 8 px (Koopa: 7 px too low, stomps 2-3 rows late); widening them or gating on `broClock` parity made the campaign worse (events 2,349 -> 2,338..2,495) because the remake's jump phase is a row off in the phase-suspect levels.
**Water, second pass (area `water2`, `evidence/water2-findings-20261111.md`, test `tests/native-water-model.test.ts`).** The "pillar" contacts above are lifts: two id 39 wrap lifts at x 364 / 460 (y 96 / 192, phase 0, spawn at x-315); rising into one is a head bump. A held A is fresh on the first control row. From native y 208 the controller does nothing, and a swimmer below the map dies at page 6. The whirlpool pull follows the landing x and native frame counter 0x09 (odd). Feet at depth >= 5 block a swimmer that is not rising; depth 0..3 lands.

Falling Mario also stomps 8 px below a rising Paratroopa's feet (3-1 row 21010); a Koopa that just lost its wings is not touched for at least 8 rows (Mario falls through it, 3-1 21014-21027, 8-2 70102-70108). Contact uses the enemy before its own step of that frame; walker half-pixel phase is per object, so the 10/11 px x edge cannot be told apart in the remake (6-2 row 48990, 8-1 row 67687 are missed at dx 11).

## Random register and the rules on it (area `enemymotion`; `src/native-random.ts`, tests `native-flying-fish`, `native-blooper`, `native-bullets`)
**RAM 0x7A7-0x7AD = 56-bit shift register**: every frame all bits move right one place, new top bit = bit 1 of byte 0 xor bit 1 of byte 1 (53,956 of 54,000 model-play rows; the other 44 are the engine 0 task 0 -> 1 load row, where it holds). **RAM 0x09 = frame counter**. It does not depend on the player, so "hidden state" that reads it differs between runs only through timing and free slots. `data/native-random-anchors.json` gives `tools/campaign-compare.ts` its value per row (recorded route: discontinuities, levels in the default capture phase shifted one row; level-checkpoint model runs: first row). Below, byte n = RAM 0x7A7+n and s = enemy slot.
**Leaping Cheep frenzy** (157 launches, 61 blocked attempts, 59 standing launches): timer RAM 0x78F counts down every frame. At 0, with s the lowest free slot: wait = [16,96,32,72][byte s+1 & 3], launch if s < limit (3 in 2-3, 4 in 7-3; 8-4 assumed 4); if all five slots are busy the timer stays 0. Speed table by Mario's unsigned speed byte RAM 0x57 (0 / below 25 / else; 25 only bounded, 24 and 31 seen; leftward speeds count as large): entry = table*4 + (byte s & 3). Speeds [.875,.3125,.375,.875, 1.75,2,1,.75, 1.875,2.125,1.5,1.25]. Offset from Mario's x: [-128,-48,64,128,-48,-80,80,112,-32,-64,128,160,-112,-64,144,104] at the entry, except for a standing Mario with (byte s+1 & 3) != 0, where the index is byte s+2 & 15 and a fish starting to his right flies left. Starts at camera 69-71 in 2-3/7-3 (8-4 from one route sample). Open: about 3% of attempts run one row late while the camera scrolls (level object read that frame, not modelled); that shifts every later launch of the run.
**Blooper**: direction RAM 0x46+s starts left; on any frame with (byte s+1 & mask) = 0 (mask 63 in 2-2, 3 in 7-2; 8-4 assumed 3) an even slot turns toward Mario and an odd slot takes Mario's motion direction RAM 0x45, also mid-stroke, not while resting at native y 184 (46/46 turns). Strokes start only when RAM 0x09 & 7 = 0 (209/209).
**Swimming Cheep generator** (2-2/7-2 water, ids 10/11; `evidence/swimcheep-findings-20261111.md`, `updateSwimGenerator`): from camera ~931 (first fish x 1217-1219) each frame, once a 32-frame timer is 0 and the lowest free slot s is below 3, a fish appears at camera + ~287 (every one of 268 free-play spawns: gap >= 32 and the lowest free slot). Byte s: id = 11 in 2-2 / 10 in 7-2 when >= 170, else the other (186/187); height index q = byte & 7, moved up (mod 8) past heights used since the bag of eight last emptied (empties when full and on each life), heights [72,56,152,88,40,104,168,120] (the same table fits each half of the runs alone; 129/135 first-life spawns). The recorded route's register reads one row off (2-2 +1, 7-2 -1), where it fits 21/21. Bob (+-15 px) is not explained by a register bit (best 70% = base rate).
**Cannons**: table of six at RAM 0x46B/0x471/0x477 (page, x, y) and 0x47D (timer), filled in load order (wraps) when x - camera is 351-354, cleared on a load. Each frame each free slot s of 0..2 reads r = byte s+1 & mask (15 in 5-x, 7 in 7-x/8-x); r < 6 picks an entry: timer > 0 counts down, else attempt and timer = 14 (9,836 of 9,838 rows).
