`native-star-flag.ts` follows PlayerEndLevel and RunStarFlagObj in native byte units. PlayerEndLevel checks task5 before the actor pass, so a task4→5 transition cannot load the next area until the following dispatch. Castle entry starts task1; it selects fireworks from the timer's last digit, then task2 trades one timer unit for50 points. Task3 raises rawY144 through113 before setting that enemy slot's EnemyIntervalTimer to6. Task4 waits for that timer and EventMusicBuffer to reach0. Fireworks use the ordinary enemy slot's ExplosionTimerCounter and ExplosionGfxCounter. RunFireworks decrements the byte timer, reloads8 on expiration and advances a cell.
